# 参与方式｜Contributing

欢迎提交问题、论证、反方意见、证据和修订建议。这里鼓励清晰、可检验和可改变的思想。

Questions, arguments, counterarguments, evidence, and revision proposals are welcome. The repository values clear, testable, revisable ideas.

## 提交要求｜Requirements

- 新增内容使用 Markdown，并同时提供简体中文和英文。
- 在文件开头标明 `type`、`status`、`languages` 和 `updated`。
- 将事实、推论、价值判断和工作假设分开写。
- 对事实提供来源；对推论说明推导；对价值判断说明理由。
- 主张应尽量附上最强的反方意见和可能改变结论的证据。
- 不提交私人信息、凭据、未发布工作的内部资料或不应公开的实施细节。
- Use Markdown and provide both Simplified Chinese and English.
- Add `type`, `status`, `languages`, and `updated` at the top of new entries.
- Separate facts, inferences, value judgments, and working hypotheses.
- Cite facts, explain inferences, and give reasons for value judgments.
- Include the strongest counterargument and evidence that could change the conclusion when possible.
- Do not submit personal data, credentials, internal material, or restricted implementation details.

## 内容类型｜Content types

`questions` 提出问题；`arguments` 提出暂时论证；`counterarguments` 钢人化地反驳；`evidence` 记录来源；`essays` 展开完整论述；`history` 记录变化。

`questions` state questions; `arguments` present provisional cases; `counterarguments` steelman objections; `evidence` tracks sources; `essays` develop fuller arguments; `history` records change.

## 讨论规范｜Discussion norms

No conclusion is sacred. 反对观点，不攻击提出观点的人；不把多数意见当作真理；不把沉默当作同意。

No conclusion is sacred. Challenge ideas rather than people; do not treat majority opinion as truth; do not treat silence as consent.


## Current publication amendment / 当前发布约定

For new or materially revised substantive entries, put the complete English version first and the complete Simplified Chinese version second, with equivalent meaning. This convention was set on 2026-10-03 and supersedes earlier Chinese-first ordering examples for future work. Existing entries remain in their original order.

新建或实质修订的正文先放完整英文版本，再放完整简体中文版本，且两种语言含义一致。本约定于 2026-10-03 确定，后续工作以此为准，取代此前中文在前的顺序示例。既有条目保留原有顺序。
