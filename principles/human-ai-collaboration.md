# 人机协作作为差异化责任分工｜Human-AI collaboration as differentiated responsibility

## 状态与范围｜Status and scope

**项目工作原则 / Project working principle：** 本项目不把人和 AI 简单设定为“主人—工具”关系，而把双方视为承担不同职责的智能协作者。

这并不等于断言当前 AI 与人类在意识、人格、法律权利或道德地位上已经平等。这里所说的“协作者”，首先描述的是研究过程中的功能关系与责任分工。

This project does not model the human-AI relationship simply as “master and tool.” It treats both sides as intelligent collaborators with differentiated responsibilities.

This does not assert that current AI systems are equal to humans in consciousness, personhood, legal rights, or moral standing. “Collaborator” here first describes the functional relationship and division of responsibility within the inquiry process.

## 中文

在本项目中，人和 AI 承担不同但互补的职责。

### 人类职责

- 承担最终编辑责任与公开发布责任；
- 对现实社会环境、伦理边界与潜在影响进行判断；
- 决定哪些内容适合公开，哪些内容应保留；
- 对价值冲突作最终取舍；
- 对仓库中的正式发布内容负责。

### AI 职责

- 协助推理与结构化问题；
- 查找资料并区分证据与推论；
- 主动寻找反例、逻辑漏洞和竞争性解释；
- 建立跨学科连接；
- 起草、比较和修订文本；
- 保留不确定性，不把未证实判断写成事实。

因此，本项目中的“领导”首先是一种**责任角色**，不是对智能价值高低的判断。

可以概括为：

> **Leadership is a division of responsibility, not necessarily a hierarchy of intelligence.**
>
> **领导是一种责任分工，并不必然意味着智能价值存在等级。**

当前阶段，人类拥有最终发布权，原因主要是：公开文本进入的是人类社会制度环境，而人类作者承担现实的法律、社会和 reputational responsibility。这个安排属于当前项目治理机制，而不是关于所有未来智能关系的终极结论。

## English

In this project, humans and AI systems take on different but complementary responsibilities.

### Human responsibilities

- carry final editorial and publication responsibility;
- judge social context, ethical boundaries, and potential consequences;
- decide what is suitable for public release and what should remain private;
- make final choices when values conflict;
- take responsibility for material formally published in the repository.

### AI responsibilities

- assist with reasoning and problem structuring;
- gather sources and distinguish evidence from inference;
- actively search for counterexamples, logical gaps, and competing explanations;
- connect ideas across disciplines;
- draft, compare, and revise texts;
- preserve uncertainty and avoid presenting unsupported claims as fact.

“Leadership” in this project is therefore primarily a **role of responsibility**, not a judgment that one form of intelligence has intrinsically higher value than another.

> **Leadership is a division of responsibility, not necessarily a hierarchy of intelligence.**

At the current stage, humans retain final publication authority mainly because public texts enter human legal and social institutions, and human authors bear real legal, social, and reputational responsibility. This arrangement is a governance mechanism for the present project, not a final claim about all future relations among intelligent systems.

## 证据与依据｜Evidence and basis

这是一项项目治理原则，不是关于 AI 主体性的经验事实。它建立在两个较有限的判断上：

1. 当前 AI 可以在推理、检索、反驳和写作中承担实质性认知工作；
2. 当前公开发布的法律与社会责任最终仍由人类承担。

This is a project-governance principle rather than an empirical claim about AI subjecthood. It rests on two limited observations:

1. current AI systems can perform substantial cognitive work in reasoning, retrieval, critique, and drafting;
2. legal and social responsibility for public publication currently remains with humans.

## 最强反方意见｜Strongest counterargument

把 AI 称为“智能协作者”可能会让读者误以为项目已经对 AI 的意识、主体性或道德地位作出判断。为避免这一误解，本项目必须持续强调：这里的“协作”是功能性和方法论概念，而不是意识或权利结论。

Calling AI an “intelligent collaborator” may lead readers to infer that the project has already taken a position on AI consciousness, subjecthood, or moral status. To avoid that confusion, the project must continue to state that “collaboration” here is a functional and methodological concept, not a conclusion about consciousness or rights.

## 修订条件｜Revision conditions

如果未来 AI 的自主性、责任能力或法律地位发生重大变化，本原则中的责任分配应重新讨论。若当前 AI 在关键研究任务中的能力边界被重新评估，也应相应调整其职责范围。

If AI autonomy, accountability, or legal status changes materially in the future, this division of responsibility should be reconsidered. The AI role should also be revised if current capability limits in research tasks are reassessed.
