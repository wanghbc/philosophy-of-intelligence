# 讨论方法（可修订）｜Discussion method (revisable)

- **No conclusion is sacred.** 每篇观点都允许修订。
- **No conclusion is sacred.** Every view may be revised.
- 区分事实、推论、价值判断与尚未检验的工作假设。
- Distinguish facts, inferences, value judgments, and untested working hypotheses.
- 对重要主张写出尽可能强的反方论证。
- State the strongest reasonable counterargument to important claims.
- 引用可追溯来源；缺少证据时明确写出不足。
- Cite traceable sources and state evidence gaps explicitly.
- 记录观点变化及其原因，不把早期文字改写成一直正确。
- Record changes and their reasons; do not rewrite early text as if it had always been correct.
- 讨论公开问题时守住公开边界。
- Keep the public boundary when discussing public questions.
