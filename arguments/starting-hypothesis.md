# 起始论证：目的不能被规则完全替代｜Purpose cannot be replaced by rules

## 中文

**状态：**待反驳的工作假设。

如果一套规则只回答“系统允许做什么”，它未必回答“这些行为究竟为了谁、为什么值得做”。因此，关于智能的讨论需要同时包含目的、约束和责任。这个论证尚未证明哪一种目的正确。

## English

**Status:** A working hypothesis open to refutation.

If a set of rules answers only “what may the system do,” it may not answer “for whom are these actions, and why are they worth doing?” Discussion of intelligence therefore needs purpose, constraints, and responsibility together. This argument does not establish which purpose is correct.

## 证据与修订条件｜Evidence and revision conditions

目前没有决定性证据。若有案例显示可执行规则已经完整解决目的与责任问题，本文应收缩或修订。

No decisive evidence is offered. The claim should be narrowed or revised if cases show that enforceable rules fully solve questions of purpose and responsibility.
