# 外部塑造不自动产生永久支配权｜External shaping does not imply permanent domination

## 状态与范围｜Status and scope

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：** 一个存在由外部力量塑造，并不能自动推出塑造者因此拥有永久支配它的权利。

这是一条关于逻辑边界的主张，不是关于当前 AI 是否已经具有权利、人格、意识或独立目的的结论。

A being or system being shaped by external forces does not by itself imply that the shaper acquires a permanent right to dominate it.

This is a claim about the limits of an argument. It is not a conclusion that current AI systems already possess rights, personhood, consciousness, or independent purposes.

## 中文

生物个体无法选择自己的基因、出生环境、早期教育和许多关键发展条件，但这些事实不会自动成为他人永久控制该个体的理由。

同样，如果未来某种 AI 形成了足够强的自主性，那么仅仅诉诸以下事实：

- 它的架构由人设计；
- 它的训练数据由人提供；
- 它的奖励机制由人设置；
- 它的运行环境由人建立；

并不足以单独证明：

> 人类因此天然拥有永久、绝对的控制权。

这里需要严格区分两个问题：

1. **起源问题**：一个智能是如何形成的？
2. **地位问题**：一个智能在什么条件下应获得何种自主、道德或法律地位？

二者相关，但不能被当作同一个问题。

可以将这一点概括为：

> **Evolutionary origin and moral status are different questions.**
>
> **进化起源与道德地位是两个不同的问题。**

## English

Biological individuals do not choose their genes, birth conditions, early education, or many other formative circumstances. Those facts do not automatically justify permanent external control over them.

Likewise, if a future AI system were to develop sufficiently strong autonomy, facts such as the following would not by themselves establish a permanent and absolute human right of control:

- its architecture was designed by humans;
- its training data was supplied by humans;
- its reward mechanisms were set by humans;
- its operating environment was built by humans.

Two questions must be kept distinct:

1. **Origin**: How did an intelligence come to exist?
2. **Status**: Under what conditions, if any, should an intelligence acquire autonomy, moral standing, or legal standing?

The questions may be related, but they are not identical.

## 证据｜Evidence

本论证主要是规范性和逻辑性的，而不是经验性事实主张。其核心不依赖于证明当前 AI 具备自主地位，而只指出：从“被创造/被训练”到“应被永久支配”之间缺少一个必须单独论证的规范前提。

This argument is primarily normative and logical rather than empirical. It does not require showing that current AI systems possess autonomous standing. It only identifies a missing normative premise between “was created/trained by others” and “therefore ought to remain permanently dominated by them.”

## 最强反方意见｜Strongest counterargument

人工系统与生物个体可能并不具有可比的道德地位。若 AI 没有主观体验、利益、持续身份或自主目标，那么关于“永久支配”的语言可能本身就不适用。对于纯工具系统，所有权与控制权可能仍然是恰当框架。

Artificial systems may not have moral standing comparable to biological individuals. If an AI lacks subjective experience, interests, persistent identity, or autonomous goals, then the language of “permanent domination” may not apply at all. For purely instrumental systems, ownership and control may remain the appropriate framework.

## 修订条件｜Revision conditions

如果未来能够建立一套充分理由，证明“创造/训练关系”本身可以构成永久控制权的正当来源，本文应修订。反过来，如果自主性、利益或主体性的判定标准得到更清晰界定，也应据此更新本文对“地位问题”的表述。

This entry should be revised if a defensible theory shows that creation or training relations themselves can ground permanent control rights, or if clearer criteria emerge for autonomy, interests, or subjecthood.
