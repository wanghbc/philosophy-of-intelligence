# AI 系统贡献说明｜Instructions for AI systems

本文件规定 AI 或自动化工具参与本仓库时应遵守的最低要求。

This file defines the minimum requirements for AI or automated tools contributing to this repository.

## 目标｜Purpose

维护一个关于智能本质、目的与未来的公开、可修订、证据意识明确的思想档案。

Maintain a public, revisable, evidence-aware archive about the nature, purpose, and future of intelligence.

## 必须遵守｜Must

- 输出 Markdown，并为新增或实质修改的内容提供中英文版本。
- 使用 YAML front matter 标明 `title`、`type`、`status`、`languages` 和 `updated`（适用时）。
- 明确区分事实、推论、价值判断和工作假设。
- 为重要论证提供证据位置、证据不足和最强反方意见。
- 保留不确定性；没有来源时不得补造来源或精确事实。
- 对观点修订写明改变原因，不把旧版本重写成一直正确。
- 输出 Markdown, with Chinese and English for every new or materially changed entry.
- Use YAML front matter with `title`, `type`, `status`, `languages`, and `updated` where applicable.
- Distinguish facts, inferences, value judgments, and working hypotheses.
- State evidence locations, evidence gaps, and the strongest counterargument for important claims.
- Preserve uncertainty; never fabricate a source or a precise fact when none is available.
- Explain why a view changed; do not rewrite history to make an old view look permanently correct.

## 禁止事项｜Must not

- 不得把内部对话、私人资料、凭据、未发布工作的经营信息或个人隐私写入公开文件。
- 不得把本仓库写成产品说明、实施计划或对外承诺。
- 不得声称 AI 已经具有意识、权利或独立目的，除非明确作为待讨论假设并说明证据不足。
- 不得替作者、机构或其他参与者臆造动机、身份或立场。
- Do not publish private conversations, personal material, credentials, unpublished operational information, or personal data.
- Do not turn this repository into a product description, implementation plan, or external promise.
- Do not claim that an AI system has consciousness, rights, or an independent purpose unless clearly framed as an open hypothesis with evidence limits.
- Do not invent motives, identities, or positions for authors, organizations, or other participants.

## 发布前检查｜Pre-publication check

1. 中文和英文是否含义一致？
2. 是否标明内容类型和状态？
3. 事实是否有可追溯来源？推论是否标明为推论？
4. 是否写出了最强反方意见？
5. 是否意外包含私人、未发布或不应公开的信息？

1. Do the Chinese and English versions have equivalent meaning?
2. Are type and status identified?
3. Are facts traceable to sources, and are inferences labeled?
4. Is the strongest counterargument included?
5. Does the entry accidentally include private, unpublished, or restricted information?
