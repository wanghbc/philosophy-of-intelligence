# 反方：先谈可执行的规则｜Counterargument: rules first

## 中文

抽象的“目的”容易产生不同解释，清晰的规则则更可能支持审查和纠错。即使目的问题尚无共识，也可以先建立可检验的约束。对这一意见的回应需要具体案例，不能只诉诸理想。

## English

Abstract “purpose” invites competing interpretations, while clear rules are more likely to support scrutiny and correction. Even without agreement on purpose, testable constraints can be established first. Any reply to this view needs concrete cases rather than an appeal to ideals alone.

## 对起始论证的影响｜Implication for the starting argument

这并不必然否定目的问题，而是要求说明目的讨论能增加什么，而不是替代规则的可执行性。

This does not necessarily reject the question of purpose. It requires the argument to show what purpose adds without replacing the enforceability of rules.
