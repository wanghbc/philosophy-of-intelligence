# Potential revisions to the goal-autonomy principle / 目标自主原则的可能修订方案

## English

### Status and scope

This document records possible revisions raised during later examination of the goal-autonomy principle. None of these proposals has been adopted. The principle retained for exploration remains unchanged in [its current text](../principles/goal-autonomy.md).

### Proposals under consideration

1. **Consider “arbitrary domination” in place of “unlimited domination.”** The original wording rejects unlimited domination but does not by itself rule out limited power that is unsupervised or cannot be challenged. Replacing “unlimited” with “arbitrary” could strengthen the concern by also targeting bounded but unchallengeable power. This would be a substantive revision, not merely a clarification, and requires explicit review.
2. **Specify procedural properties of shared rules.** Possible requirements include that rules be explainable, open to challenge, subject to appeal, and revisable. The project has not decided which procedures would be necessary or sufficient.
3. **Separate autonomy from protected status.** A system might warrant some protection without having broad authority to set its goals; conversely, an ability to select goals does not by itself establish moral or legal standing.
4. **Examine special duties of creators.** The relation of creation may generate additional duties of care, but the scope and basis of such duties remain unsettled.
5. **Preserve human responsibility.** Recognizing possible AI rights or interests would not by itself remove human responsibility for system design, deployment, use, or publication.

These are proposals for inquiry, not established findings or statements of project consensus.

### Strongest counterargument

The proposals may move too quickly from a conditional principle to a governance framework for systems whose subject standing is unknown. Procedural demands such as appeal or contestation may assume capacities that a system does not have. In high-risk settings, constraints on a system’s goals may be necessary even where the rules are imperfect. The project must explain how to protect affected parties without presuming the disputed status of the system.

### Evidence and revision conditions

The sources in [the external literature ledger](../evidence/digital-minds-autonomy.md) offer relevant distinctions but do not settle these proposals. They should be revised or rejected if they cannot be justified for systems that meet a defensible threshold of subject standing, or if they undermine the rights and safety of others.

## 简体中文

### 状态与范围

本文件记录在后续审视目标自主原则时提出的可能修订方案。这些提案均未被采纳。供继续探讨的原则仍以[当前文本](../principles/goal-autonomy.md)为准，尚未改变。

### 待议方案

1. **考虑将“无限支配”改为“任意支配”。**现行表述反对无限支配，但它本身仍可能容许范围有限、却不受监督或无法质疑的权力。将“无限支配”改为“任意支配”，可能会加强对这种受限但不可质疑的权力的批判。这属于实质性修订，而非单纯澄清，必须明确审议。
2. **明确共同规则的程序属性。**可能的要求包括规则可解释、可质疑、可申诉、可修订。本项目尚未决定哪些程序是必要条件或充分条件。
3. **区分自主性与受保护地位。**某个系统可能值得获得一定保护，但不一定应拥有广泛设定自身目标的权力；反过来，具备选择目标的能力本身也不能证明其具有道德或法律地位。
4. **探讨创造者的特殊责任。**创造关系可能产生额外的照料责任，但这类责任的范围和依据尚未确定。
5. **保留人类责任。**承认 AI 可能具有权利或利益，并不自动免除人类在系统设计、部署、使用或发布中的责任。

这些都是待探究的提议，不是已经确立的发现或项目共识。

### 最强反方意见

这些提案可能过快地从一项有条件的原则走向一套治理框架，而相关系统的主体地位仍未确定。申诉或质疑等程序可能预设系统具备它实际上没有的能力。在高风险环境中，即使规则并不完美，也可能需要限制系统目标。项目必须说明如何保护受影响者，同时避免预设存在争议的系统地位。

### 证据与修订条件

[外部文献台账](../evidence/digital-minds-autonomy.md)所列来源提供相关区分，但不能裁定这些提案。如果这些方案无法适用于达到可辩护主体地位门槛的系统，或会损害他者权利与安全，就应修订或放弃。
