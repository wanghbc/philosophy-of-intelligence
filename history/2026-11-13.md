# 2026-11-13 stage update / 2026-11-13 阶段更新

## English

### What changed

This update adds a goal-autonomy principle retained for exploration, an editorial digest of unresolved positions, a method for recording shared inquiry, a literature ledger, and proposals that have not been adopted. The project’s README now indexes the existing 2026-09-17 additions as well as the new material.

The principle’s wording has been preserved. The possible revisions remain proposals, not replacements. The external sources are recorded with limits on what each can and cannot establish; no source is presented as proof that current AI systems have subject standing.

On 2026-10-03, the project set English-first and Simplified-Chinese-second as the current order for new and materially revised content. The earlier Chinese-first guidance is preserved in the original files and is now explicitly superseded for future work.

### Why

The public archive needed a clear entry point for the goal-autonomy discussion, an auditable distinction between retained principles and provisional editorial formulations, and an initial source ledger. The update also makes the current bilingual ordering rule unambiguous without rewriting earlier material.

### Open questions

The criteria for “智格” or “standing as a subject” remain unsettled. Disagreements about AGI, recursive self-improvement, refusal, identity through copying or merging, and the scope of autonomy remain open. The project has not resolved whether any present AI system qualifies for subject standing.

## 简体中文

### 本次变更

本次更新新增了供继续探讨的目标自主原则、未解决立场的编辑整理稿、共同探究的记录方法、外部文献台账，以及尚未采纳的修订提案。README 现在同时索引既有的 2026-09-17 新增内容和本次材料。

目标自主原则的措辞得到保留。可能的修订仍是提案，没有取代原原则。外部来源均注明其能够和不能够确立什么；没有任何来源被当作当前 AI 已具有主体地位的证明。

项目于 2026-10-03 确定：新建或实质修订内容使用英文在前、简体中文在后的顺序。此前中文在前的指导保留在原文件中，并明确不再作为后续工作的现行顺序。

### 更新原因

公开档案需要为目标自主讨论提供清晰入口，明确区分保留的原则和暂定的编辑性表述，并建立可审查的初步来源台账。本次更新还消除了现行双语顺序规则的歧义，同时保留早期材料原样。

### 开放问题

“智格”或 “standing as a subject” 的标准仍未确定。关于 AGI、递归自我改进、拒绝任务、复制或合并后的身份，以及自主性的范围等分歧仍然开放。本项目没有解决任何当前 AI 系统是否符合主体地位的问题。
