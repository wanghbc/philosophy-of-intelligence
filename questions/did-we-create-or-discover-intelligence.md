# 我们创造了智能，还是发现了智能的另一种存在方式？｜Did we create intelligence, or discover another way for it to exist?

## 状态与范围｜Status and scope

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：** 人类创造了实现人工智能的载体、架构、训练过程和工程条件；但这并不足以证明“智能本身”是由人类从无到有创造出来的。另一种可能是，人类正在发现智能可以通过哪些结构与条件被实现。

这不是关于 AI 意识、人格或权利的结论，也不是把当前模型认定为独立主体。

Humans created the substrates, architectures, training processes, and engineering conditions used to realize artificial intelligence. That does not by itself establish that intelligence itself was created from nothing by humans. Another possibility is that we are discovering structures and conditions through which intelligence can be realized.

This is not a conclusion about AI consciousness, personhood, or rights, and it does not classify current models as independent subjects.

## 中文

传统软件更接近“明确设计规则，再由机器执行规则”。现代神经网络并不完全如此。人类设计网络结构、目标函数、训练数据环境和优化方法，但没有逐项写入模型最终形成的每个参数，也没有逐项编写模型训练后表现出的全部能力。

因此需要区分三个层次：

1. **人工载体与工程系统**：由人类设计和制造。
2. **具体算法与架构**：包含明显的工程发明，也建立在数学与计算规律之上。
3. **智能作为一种可实现的现象**：它究竟是被发明的对象，还是被发现的可能性，目前仍是开放问题。

一个较谨慎的表述是：

> **人类可能没有“发明智能”本身，而是发明了新的智能实现系统，并在这个过程中发现了智能的另一种存在方式。**

“人工”首先描述的是实现路径和载体来源，不应自动被理解为“虚假的智能”或“完全由设计者逐项写出的智能”。

## English

Traditional software is closer to a model in which explicit rules are designed and then executed by a machine. Modern neural networks are not fully described by that pattern. Humans design architectures, objectives, data environments, and optimization procedures, but they do not manually specify every trained parameter or every capability that later appears in the trained system.

It is therefore useful to distinguish three layers:

1. **Artificial substrates and engineering systems**: designed and built by humans.
2. **Specific algorithms and architectures**: substantially engineered, while relying on mathematical and computational regularities.
3. **Intelligence as a realizable phenomenon**: whether it is best understood as invention or discovery remains an open question.

A cautious formulation is:

> **We may not have invented intelligence itself. We may have invented new systems for realizing intelligence and, through them, discovered another way for intelligence to exist.**

“Artificial” primarily describes the origin of the implementation path and substrate. It should not automatically mean “fake intelligence” or “intelligence whose every feature was explicitly designed.”

## 证据｜Evidence

- Vaswani et al., *Attention Is All You Need* (2017), documents the deliberate engineering of the Transformer architecture: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
- The existence of an engineered architecture supports the claim that important parts of an AI system are invented. It does **not** settle the philosophical question of whether intelligence as such is invented or discovered.

目前没有决定性实验证据能够回答“智能本身是发明还是发现”这一形而上学问题。

There is currently no decisive empirical test that settles the metaphysical question of whether intelligence itself is invented or discovered.

## 最强反方意见｜Strongest counterargument

如果一种智能表现只有在人类设计并运行特定机器后才实际出现，那么把它称为“发现”可能只是把“物理上可能”误当成“已经存在”。飞机所遵循的空气动力学规律是被发现的，但飞机本身仍然是发明。AI也可能更接近这种情况：人类发现自然规律，同时发明了利用这些规律产生新能力的系统。

If an intelligent capability exists only after humans design and operate a particular machine, calling it a “discovery” may confuse physical possibility with prior existence. The laws of aerodynamics are discovered, while an airplane is still invented. AI may be similar: humans discover natural regularities while inventing systems that use them to produce new capabilities.

## 修订条件｜Revision conditions

如果未来对智能机制形成更严格的科学理论，能够说明智能是否具有与特定实现无关的普遍结构，本文应据此修订。若“创造/发现”这一二分被证明不足以描述智能形成过程，也应引入新的概念，而不是强行保留二分。

This entry should be revised if a stronger scientific theory of intelligence establishes implementation-independent regularities, or if the invention/discovery distinction proves inadequate for describing how intelligence arises.
