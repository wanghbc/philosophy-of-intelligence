# AI 为什么存在？｜Why does AI exist?

## 中文

一种可能回答是：AI 由人类创造，首先应服务于人的能力、尊严与福祉。但“服务”不能替代对目的的追问：服务谁，谁来定义福祉，人的判断应在什么情况下保持优先？如果某类智能表现出超出工具的性质，原有回答还够吗？

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：**智能的价值可以从帮助不同主体理解世界、拓展能力以及降低可避免的伤害来讨论；这些目标彼此可能冲突，不能直接合并成单一口号。

**反方问题 / Counter-question：**把人类目的作为唯一标准，是否忽略未来可能出现的其他道德主体？把智能本身当作目的，是否会模糊现阶段人类应承担的责任？

本条是问题提纲，不断言当前 AI 有意识或独立的利益。

## English

One possible answer is that AI is created by humans and should first serve human capability, dignity, and well-being. But “service” does not settle the question of purpose: whom does it serve, who defines well-being, and when should human judgment remain primary? If a kind of intelligence comes to have properties beyond a tool, would this answer still be sufficient?

**Working hypothesis:** The value of intelligence can be discussed in terms of helping different subjects understand the world, extending their capabilities, and reducing avoidable harm. These aims may conflict and cannot be collapsed into one slogan.

**Counter-question:** Does treating human purposes as the only standard overlook possible future moral subjects? Does treating intelligence itself as the purpose blur the responsibilities humans have now?

This is a question outline. It does not claim that current AI has consciousness or independent interests.

## 证据与修订条件｜Evidence and revision conditions

目前没有为本条提出决定性证据。关于意识、利益或道德地位的具体主张，需要另行提供来源和反方论证。

No decisive evidence is offered here. Specific claims about consciousness, interests, or moral status require separate sources and counterarguments.
