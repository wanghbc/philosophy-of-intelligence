# 人机共生意味着什么？｜What would human–AI coexistence mean?

## 中文

“共生”至少包含三个层面：人借助 AI 扩展能力；人仍能质疑与纠正 AI；社会能够讨论由这些关系产生的权力与责任。这个词不预设 AI 具有意识，也不预设人和 AI 应享有相同的道德地位。

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：**好的关系应让人的自主判断能力和问责机制得到维持。

**反方问题 / Counter-question：**高度依赖是否使自主判断逐渐弱化？若未来智能的性质改变，现有关系是否需要重新定义？

## English

“Coexistence” has at least three dimensions: people use AI to extend their capabilities; people remain able to question and correct AI; and society can discuss the power and responsibility produced by these relationships. The term does not assume that AI is conscious or that people and AI should have equal moral status.

**Working hypothesis:** A good relationship should preserve human capacity for independent judgment and mechanisms of accountability.

**Counter-question:** Could heavy dependence gradually weaken independent judgment? If the nature of intelligence changes, would the relationship need to be redefined?

## 证据与修订条件｜Evidence and revision conditions

需要通过现实使用案例和可核验研究检验“扩展能力”与“削弱自主性”是否同时发生，以及如何权衡。

Real cases and verifiable research are needed to test whether capability extension and loss of autonomy occur together and how they should be weighed.
