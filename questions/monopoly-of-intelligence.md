# 智能是否应被垄断？｜Should intelligence be monopolized?

## 中文

当强大的智能能力集中在少数主体手中，谁可以获取知识、参与决定和质疑系统，就成为哲学与公共治理问题。“垄断”在此是待澄清的问题词，不是对某种结构已经成立的实证判断。

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：**能力分配、审查权和问责能力值得共同讨论。

**反方问题 / Counter-question：**集中是否在某些风险场景更便于治理？分散是否也可能扩大伤害？需要先定义何为“集中”，再用可核验证据比较现实后果。

## English

When powerful intelligence is concentrated in the hands of a few actors, who may access knowledge, participate in decisions, and question the system becomes a philosophical and public-governance question. “Monopoly” is a term to clarify here, not an empirical claim that a particular structure already exists.

**Working hypothesis:** The distribution of capability, the power to scrutinize, and the ability to hold systems accountable should be considered together.

**Counter-question:** Is concentration easier to govern in some high-risk settings? Could distribution also expand harm? We need to define “concentration” before comparing real-world consequences with verifiable evidence.

## 证据与修订条件｜Evidence and revision conditions

需要分别考察获取、审查、决策参与和责任承担，而不能用单一指标代表“垄断”。新的证据应能改变这些维度中的至少一个判断。

Access, scrutiny, participation in decisions, and responsibility should be examined separately rather than represented by one measure of “monopoly.” New evidence should be able to change at least one of these judgments.
