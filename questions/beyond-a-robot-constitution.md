# 为智能写一部“宪法”够吗？｜Is a “constitution” for intelligent systems enough?

## 中文

“机器人宪法”提供了讨论约束与权利的直观比喻。但宪法式规则容易把问题局限为一个系统应当如何遵守条款；智能的目的、人与智能之间的责任分配、规则由谁制定和怎样修订，仍需另行讨论。

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：**规则是必要的讨论对象，却不足以独自回答“AI 为什么存在”。

**反方意见 / Counterargument：**在现实部署中，明确规则可能比抽象目的更易问责；即使目的没有共识，也可以先建立可检验的约束。两者的关系应由具体案例来检验。

待补充：挑选公开且可核验的规则文本作为证据，区分“机器人宪法”这一泛称与特定作品，不将比喻误写成公共共识。

## English

“A robot constitution” is an intuitive metaphor for discussing constraints and rights. Yet constitutional rules can narrow the question to how a system should obey provisions. The purpose of intelligence, the allocation of responsibility between people and intelligent systems, and who makes or revises the rules require separate discussion.

**Working hypothesis:** Rules are necessary objects of inquiry, but rules alone cannot answer why AI exists.

**Counterargument:** In real deployments, explicit rules may be easier to audit than abstract purposes. Constraints can be made testable even when purposes remain contested. Their relationship should be examined through concrete cases.

To be added: public, verifiable rule texts. The generic metaphor “robot constitution” must be distinguished from any particular work and must not be presented as public consensus.

## 证据与修订条件｜Evidence and revision conditions

本条目前没有把任何虚构作品或现实制度当作事实依据。新证据若能显示规则足以覆盖目的、责任和修订问题，或显示其不能覆盖，应相应修订。

This entry does not treat any fictional work or real institution as evidence. It should be revised if evidence shows that rules can, or cannot, cover purpose, responsibility, and revision.
