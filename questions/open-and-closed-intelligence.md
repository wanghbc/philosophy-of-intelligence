# 开源与闭源之间如何判断？｜How should openness and closure be judged?

## 中文

开放有助于审查、参与和知识传播；限制访问有时可能减少滥用并方便承担责任。两种理由不能简单互相抵消，也不能从一种发布方式直接推断组织的真实动机。

**工作假设 / Working hypothesis：**评价应具体到能力、可访问范围、潜在伤害、监督手段以及由谁承担风险，而非只给“开放”或“封闭”贴道德标签。

**反方问题 / Counter-question：**限制获取是否把话语权过度交给少数机构？完全开放是否把成本转嫁给无法防护的人？如何观察并修正选择的后果？

本条没有对某一人物、组织或近期争论做事实判定；涉及具体主张时应补充原始来源与上下文。

## English

Openness can support scrutiny, participation, and the spread of knowledge. Restricting access can sometimes reduce misuse and make responsibility easier to assign. These reasons cannot simply cancel each other out, and a release model does not by itself reveal an organization’s motives.

**Working hypothesis:** Evaluation should consider capabilities, access boundaries, potential harms, oversight, and who bears the risks, rather than assigning a moral label to “open” or “closed.”

**Counter-question:** Do access restrictions give too much voice to a small number of institutions? Does complete openness transfer costs to people who cannot protect themselves? How should consequences be observed and choices revised?

This entry makes no factual determination about a particular person, organization, or current controversy. Specific claims require primary sources and context.

## 证据与修订条件｜Evidence and revision conditions

需要比较不同发布方式的实际后果，而不是只比较口号。若新的公开证据显示风险、监督或参与效果明显不同，应更新本文。

The practical consequences of different release models must be compared rather than slogans. Update this entry when public evidence shows materially different effects on risk, oversight, or participation.
